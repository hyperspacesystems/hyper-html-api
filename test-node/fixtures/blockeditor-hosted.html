Sunday dinner

Six friends are coming this Sunday at six for supper on the porch. Keep things simple and finish prep early.

Menu

  • Roast chicken with lemon and herbs.
  • Crispy potatoes with sea salt.
  • Green salad with toasted walnuts.
  • Warm peach crisp with cream.

Before guests arrive

  • Wipe the porch table
  • Set extra chairs on the porch
  • Fill two water pitchers
  • Light the patio lanterns
🍋 Salt the chicken an hour early so the skin roasts crisp.
  1. 3:30 pm: Salt the chicken and leave it out.
  2. 4:30 pm: Roast the chicken and potatoes.
  3. 5:15 pm: Assemble the peach crisp.
  4. 5:45 pm: Rest the chicken, bake the crisp.

Peach crisp

Toss sliced peaches with lemon juice and sugar.

Cover with oats, flour, brown sugar, and butter.

Bake at 375 degrees until golden.
